The default CPU decoder uses thread-local output buffers and ordered writes. Fixed-width fields are restored from columns while traversal nodes stream through the grammar expander.

07CPU decompression speed

Inputs are preloaded into page cache; decompression writes to /dev/null to isolate compute throughput. GFAz is fastest on five of six datasets and reaches 5.4 GiB/s on HPRC v2.0.

12Current boundary

Whole-genome PAV is not solved yet. Its uncompressed node-set/window index—not traversal decoding—exceeds memory on HPRC v2.0/v2.1. Compressing that working index is the next target.

A pangenome graph does not have to be unpacked to be queried.

GFAz turns the largest part of GFA into a representation that is simultaneously compact, fast to decode, and useful for direct analysis.

  • 18–84× lossless compression
  • up to 5.4 GiB/s CPU decompression
  • one to three orders of magnitude faster analysis
